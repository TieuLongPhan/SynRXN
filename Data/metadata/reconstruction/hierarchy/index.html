Redistribution decision
The 11,011 rows are an author-published, curated reaction-structure dataset, rather than a private or unavailable Reaxys dump. Goodman and Lam (2024) explicitly used the original Li/Tang (2022) training reactions, published the processed file, and described its filtering in the supporting information. The original 2022 paper explicitly publishes the training/validation repository in its Data Availability statement and carries a CC BY-NC 3.0 article notice. All 11,011 local rows exactly match the Goodman source. An independent structure join matches 11,009 original training records; two rows retain connectivity and product identity but differ in reactant stereochemical notation. Public availability and article licensing are therefore established. However, an explicit downstream grant for these external reaction files, or the applicable author/Elsevier agreement covering redistribution of the extracted reaction compilation, has not been located. The original repository MIT notice refers to software and documentation and retains another project copyright; the Goodman repository has no dataset-specific licence file.
